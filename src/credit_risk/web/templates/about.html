About — how it works

A transparent, auditable credit decisioning pipeline — from raw applicant data to a graded decision with reason codes and a credit memo.

How a decision is made

Every applicant flows through the same seven-step pipeline, so decisions stay consistent, explainable, and reproducible.

StepStageWhat happens
1 Validate Inbound features are schema-checked and range-validated; malformed records are rejected before scoring.
2 Engineer features & standardise The 12 raw inputs are transformed and standardised onto the scale the model was trained on.
3 Model PD The trained model produces a raw probability of default for the applicant.
4 Calibrate The raw score is calibrated so the reported PD reflects real-world default frequency.
5 Grade + policy The calibrated PD is mapped to a risk grade and decision via policy bands, with a human-review margin around the cut-offs.
6 Reason codes Per-feature contributions are surfaced as the factors that increase and reduce risk for this applicant.
7 Credit memo A plain-language memo summarises the decision and its drivers for the underwriter's file.

Applicants that fall within the human-review margin around a policy cut-off are flagged for manual review rather than auto-decided.
